1. The pros of agile development are the deadlines set by the sprint method which promotes completing work more efficiently and the ability to adjust and adapt during the sprint. The cons of agile development are less continuity when it comes to completing big projects, and potentially bogging down developers' schedules with meetings and updates for sprints.
2. The Scrum Master's job is to manage the sprints and make sure the team's work is completable during the sprint. They lead standups, retrospectives, and planning sessions. They are basically sprint managers. The Product Owner is the connection from the dev team to the client. They create/provide input for what tickets need to be put in the backlog and help guide the vision of the project, not the sprint process.
3. The 3 types of meetings that need to happen during the sprint cycle are standups, sprint planning, and sprint review (retrospectives). In standups, developers share what their progress is on the Jiras they are working on and if they have any blockers/need help. In the Sprint planning sessions, Product Owner and Scrum Master work with developers to attain a sprint plan that is achievable. They discuss the Jiras they are taking on. They also discuss sizing of the work. In the sprint retrospective, the scrum master gathers feedback from developers and product to make the next sprint better. They discuss what was completed and what will spill over. They also discuss if there are any changes that members of the team want to propse for future sprints.
